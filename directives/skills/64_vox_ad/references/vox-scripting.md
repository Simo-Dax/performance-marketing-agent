# VOX scripting — write nouns you can cut out of paper

The entire format rests on one writing law: **every sentence names concrete, drawable
things.** The beat map turns nouns into paper objects; a sentence with no cuttable noun
gives the screen nothing to do and is a rewrite.

Test each line: read it and list what pops. "We make advertising easier" pops NOTHING —
rewrite. "Meta picks one ad and burns it out in six days" pops the Meta logo, an ad
card, flames, a calendar 6. That is a VOX line.

## Shape

- **Length:** the user's target from intake, default 25s. Budget ~2.2 words/sec
  (25s ≈ 55 words, 15s ≈ 33, 40s ≈ 88). Err short — air beats rush.
- **Structure (3–5 sentences):** a familiar pain stated with objects → agitate/deepen
  it → the TURN names the brand/product → the payoff → the spoken CTA line (which
  becomes the ad's only banner).
- Short punchy sentences. Numbers are gold — a spoken number becomes a badge, a
  calendar, a stat that pops.
- The turn must NAME the product/brand out loud: the word-to-object law puts the real
  product or logo on that exact word.
- Second person or neutral voice; never first person.

## Grounding

- Mine the VOC for the highest-intensity pain and carry at least one verbatim customer
  phrase (or the product's named mechanism). Clear every forbidden phrase in the brand
  memory. Foundation Pack (when present) decides WHAT to say per R.9.
- Ask the reader's world: which objects are FAMILIAR to this audience? (Media buyers:
  feed cards, CTR arrows, notification badges. DTC: the product, stars, carts, coins.)

## ElevenLabs hand-off

One voice, one continuous read, no music baked in. Direction: direct, confident,
conversational — never salesy. **Ask for a clear breath after the problem's last
sentence** — that pause is the ad's pivot and gives the sync step room. The file lands
in `<work>/audio/`; the REAL recorded length becomes the film length.

## Swipe scripts (both shipped)

1. Andromeda (23.6s, platform-villain arc):
   "Your winners used to last months. Then Andromeda happened. Now Meta picks one ad,
   feeds it everything, and burns it out in six days. And it never stops asking for
   more. the agent is the loop that keeps up. Research. Build. Diagnose. Multiply.
   Never run out of ads again."
2. Same tools (17.6s, sameness-vs-input arc):
   "Everyone's using the same AI tools. So everyone's ads look the same. The tool never
   asked about your brand. It never read a single customer review. the agent reads your
   brand rules and your customers' exact words first. Then it builds ads only you could
   run."
