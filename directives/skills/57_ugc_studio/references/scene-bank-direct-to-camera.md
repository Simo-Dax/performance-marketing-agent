# Scene Bank — DIRECT-TO-CAMERA NARRATIVE (UGC Studio format 04), full teardown of one winning ad

The cold-traffic workhorse: one creator talking to the lens through a problem-first arc —
hook → belief-break → industry failure → THE TURN → proof suite → identity close. Seeded
from ONE member-supplied winning ad (torn down word-for-word and frame-by-frame on
2026-07-10); **the second-winner slot is OPEN** — the next on-format ad the member banks
(ideally a FIRST-PERSON confession story: "I struggled with X for years…", product absent
until the turn) completes the pair with the contrasting narrative voice. Same fill rules as
the other banks: shells recreate the source scene shapes; fills swap product, category,
claims, and settings; one shell serves at most ONE scene per ad; record `source_shell`
(N01… or "synthesized").

This is the format the studio's variant fan-out was designed around: the 4-variant batch
(4 unique hooks + the insert-count ladder on a shared body) IS this format's batch mode,
specified in this skill's own `variant-fanout.md`, and this bank's beats map straight onto
hook / spine / insert generations.

THE DURATION LAW: every generation is an integer 4–9 seconds. THE CAPTION LAW: the source
burns phrase-synced captions and a "4g fiber ✅" style callout sticker — source-only design
layers; our renders ship CLEAN. THE CLAIMS LAW: the source's brand, claims, and label facts
("Mid-Day Squares", "protein, fiber, clean functional ingredients", the nutrition panel)
are its own — fills use the member's sanctioned claims, and label close-ups render ONLY
from the member's real packaging photos (the product law).

## THE RHYTHM CARD (measured 2026-07-10 — the QC source of truth for this format)

Ad N (Mid-Day Squares, 35.88s, 143 words, 3.98 wps): **25 distinct shots, median 1.13s,
shortest 0.60s, longest 4.23s.**

- **Cuts land on words.** 14 of 24 cuts are mid-sentence, 7 literally MID-WORD (inside
  "ridiculously", "balanced", "clean", "while", "brain", "indulgence", "doesn't"). One
  continuous VO rides every cut; all inserts are MUTE under it; a music/ambience bed fills
  speech gaps (measured −19dB in pauses — never silence; note: our music stays editor-side,
  so our seams rely on clip room tone + the VO).
- **ACCELERATION LAW:** 4 shots averaging 2.76s before THE TURN, then 21 shots averaging
  1.18s after it — cut density DOUBLES the moment the product enters (turn at 31%, inside
  the 25–35% law).
- **Spine:** the walking spine supplies 12 of 25 shots across 5 separate returns (opens the
  ad, returns at 15.87/20.37/29.63, holds the final button 34.0–35.88). Spine take joins
  hide in 0.2–0.4s sentence-gap pauses with matched energy/direction (2.40, 8.53, 31.43).
- **Insert suites:** runs of 3 quick shots at 0.6–1.2s, one per phrase — sensory triple
  cuts ON "looks luxurious / tastes rich / satisfying", packet color-run cuts ON "no guilt
  / no crash / no compromise" (0.63/0.60/0.73s).
- **Product callback structure:** cameo ≤2.4s in the hook (no pitch), VANISHES for 8.6s of
  problem, snap-return at THE TURN, final cameo raised-to-lens on the button held 1.88s
  with a ~0.6s tail after the last word.
- **The two-outfit interleave** (white-tee vs black-knit butt-cut mid-sentence) requires
  one body ref PER outfit — without a second ref, vary lighting/framing instead.
- **The caption caveat:** the source's caption+sticker layer did rhythmic work (a sticker
  pop acts as a pseudo-cut) — our renders ship clean, so our edit needs every real cut
  MORE than the source did.

**QC targets the stitcher reads:** median ≤1.5s, no shot >4s except the button close,
~0.7 cuts/sec overall, acceleration at the turn.

## The systems this ad banks

- **The walking spine.** The talking beats all happen mid-walk through a sunny
  neighborhood — constant motion, background sliding past, retention for free. Multiple
  walk takes (and even two outfits) read as one continuous rant because the energy and
  direction match. This is the scene ladder solved with ONE device.
- **Tease, vanish, return.** The product cameos beside his face during the question hook
  (2 seconds, no pitch), disappears for the entire problem section, and snaps back at the
  turn. The cameo plants the payoff; the absence makes the problem feel honest; the return
  lands as the answer. Fills may use the cameo or keep the product fully hidden until the
  turn — never pitch before it.
- **The turn is a sentence, not a section.** Five words ("a whole different game") and the
  ad is already proving. No feature dump at the turn — the proof suite does it.
- **The proof suite: every claim type gets its own insert.** Sensory claim → food macro;
  mechanism claim → the real label; lifestyle claim → gym b-roll; indulgence claim → the
  eating beat; tagline → the packet color run; range → the box stack. The VO never says
  anything the picture isn't simultaneously showing. (Per the render laws, the
  fidelity-critical inserts — label, bitten state, variant run, stack — are still-inserts
  from the member's real photos, not rendered gambles.)
- **Word-action locks ×3.** "Looks luxurious / tastes rich / satisfying" cut on each
  phrase; the packet color changes exactly on "no guilt / no crash / no compromise"; the
  label macro lands on "protein, fiber". At fill, at least two beats must be word-locked —
  and the locks are EDIT-side guarantees (cuts and overlays placed at word onsets on the
  whisper timeline), never render promises.
- **Second-person editorial voice.** No confession, no "I" story — the narrative is YOURS
  ("your cheat snack… your goals… your brain"). This is the DISRUPT-flavored narrative
  voice; the first-person confession variant is the open second-winner slot.
- **The identity close + button.** No price, no offer, no "link below" — the close
  reframes the buyer's identity ("enjoying it smarter") and lands a 7-word button with the
  product at the lens. The offer rides the ad copy.
- **Two outfits as authenticity.** The white-tee eating/walking beats against the
  black-knit spine read as "this is my actual life across days", the same trick the
  before/after bank uses for time-jumps. HARD REQUIREMENT: a second outfit needs its own
  body reference at intake — without it, the fill keeps one outfit and varies lighting,
  location, and framing instead.

## Scene shells

House prompt shape applies (less-is-more; the scene line + one emotion word + the exact
quote; product size inline when on screen; "No on-screen text or captions." always).

Every shell carries its render-audit verdict (2026-07-10): **GREEN** renders as written;
**YELLOW(adaptation)** renders with the stated adaptation; **RED(replacement)** never
renders as written — the beat is replaced through another lane, usually a still-insert
built from the member's real photos. The laws cited live in `render-laws.md`.

**Shell N01 — THE QUESTION HOOK + CAMEO.**
> Walking down a sunny residential sidewalk, handheld selfie. He raises [PRODUCT (size)]
> beside his face for the length of the question, then lowers it out of frame. Delivery:
> conspiratorial what-if. He says: "What if your [GUILTY THING] was actually [THE
> UNEXPECTED GOOD OUTCOME]?"

**VERDICT: GREEN** — a hold is the bottom rung of the manipulation ladder, one product ref
per gen is respected, and the clean bare face on the sunny walk makes this the natural
identity-checkpoint generation.

NOTE: question hooks are the weaker verified family — the cameo + contrarian body is what
earns it here; a fill may swap in an offer-led or confession hook line and keep the cameo.

**Shell N02 — THE BELIEF-BREAK.**
> Same walk, product gone. One line that snaps the category's rule. Delivery: matter-of-
> fact. He says: "[THE CATEGORY BELIEF] doesn't have to [THE ACCEPTED PAIN]."

**VERDICT: GREEN** — product-free spine talking beat, no macro, no manipulation; renders
as written (merged forward with N01, see the generation table).

**Shell N03 — THE INDUSTRY-FAILURE RANT (multi-beat, product-free).**
> Same walk, bigger gestures, air quotes on the category's buzzwords. Delivery: fed up,
> building. He says: "Here's the deal, most [CATEGORY PRODUCTS] either [FAILURE 1] or
> [FAILURE 2 with a concrete detail]. And don't get me started on [THE WORST OFFENDER]."

**VERDICT: GREEN** — one continuous product-free spine take; its cut density is
manufactured edit-side (interior dead-air trims + crop punch-ins), so the gen plans ONE
clear performance, no internal sub-shot gymnastics needed.

COMPLIANCE: attack the category generically, never a named competitor.

**Shell N04 — THE TURN.**
> Same walk; [PRODUCT (size)] snaps back up beside his face. Delivery: the grin of someone
> holding the answer. He says: "But [PRODUCT NAME]? A whole different game."

**VERDICT: GREEN** — one clear action (the raise), one product ref attached; no state
change mid-shot beyond the raise itself.

**Shell N05 — THE SENSORY TRIPLE (insert, word-locked).**
> Kitchen close-ups, one macro per phrase, hard cuts: [t1] the product held ([SENSORY 1]),
> [t2] bitten/opened ([SENSORY 2]), [t3] the cross-section/detail ([SENSORY 3]). VO
> carries: "It [SENSORY 1], [SENSORY 2], and is [SENSORY 3]."

**VERDICT: YELLOW(adaptation)** — the bite is a top rung of the product-manipulation
ladder (and macro-adjacent to the teeth ban): only the HELD macro renders in-gen with the
product ref; the bitten state and the cross-section become still-inserts from the member's
real open/eaten-state photos, flashed at the word onsets. The triple survives intact —
one gen sub-shot + two still flashes.

**Shell N06 — THE MECHANISM + LABEL.**
> One walking beat ("But here's the twist"), HARD CUT to the label — the member's actual
> nutrition-label photo as a STILL-INSERT, the named facts legible. VO: "every [UNIT] is
> perfectly balanced with [THE 2–3 REAL LABEL FACTS]."

**VERDICT: RED(replacement)** — the nutrition-label macro is a fine-print macro ban and is
NEVER rendered; it is a still-insert built from the member's real label photo, punched in
as an overlay at the word onset ("protein, fiber"). The walking twist line survives as a
generation; only the label beat is replaced.

**Shell N07 — THE REFRAME SUITE (insert b-rolls, VO carries).**
> [t1] The character mid-[LIFESTYLE ACTION: lift / run / work] in a matching setting.
> [t2] HARD CUT: the character (second outfit) enjoying the product, eyes-closed beat.
> VO: "This isn't about [THE FAKE PROMISE], it's about [THE REAL PROMISE] while still
> [THE PLEASURE KEPT]."

**VERDICT: YELLOW(adaptation)** — three laws bite at once: (1) the eating beat is a top
rung of the manipulation ladder — it plays as an eyes-closed enjoyment beat with the
product HELD (no on-camera bite) unless a real bitten-state photo backs a still-insert;
(2) the second outfit REQUIRES its own body reference or the beat collapses to the first
outfit with lighting/framing variation; (3) any gym extras render from-behind/silhouette
only — never two distinct identities in one generation.

**Shell N08 — THE TRIPLE TAG (insert, word-locked).**
> A palm holds [VARIANT 1 (size)]; HARD CUT to [VARIANT 2]; HARD CUT to [VARIANT 3] — one
> packet per phrase. VO: "No [PAIN 1], no [PAIN 2], no [PAIN 3]."

**VERDICT: RED(replacement)** — three variants in one generation breaks the
one-product-ref-per-gen law (and each variant needs its own real photo anyway). The color
run rebuilds as a still-flash suite: one 2K palm-held still per variant's real photo, cut
on the word onsets at the measured 0.63/0.60/0.73s flash lengths. Nothing is lost — the
suite keeps all three pictures.

**Shell N09 — THE IDENTITY CLOSE.**
> The full range stacked ([THE BOX STACK, real packaging]), then back on the walk.
> Delivery: settled, certain. He says: "[PRODUCT] doesn't just fit your lifestyle, [it
> elevates it / the identity line]. [THE REFRAME MAXIM]."

**VERDICT: YELLOW(adaptation)** — the box stack is a multi-variant, label-legible beat
(one-ref law + every-variant-photo law): it routes to a still-insert composed from the
member's real packaging photos, overlaid on the walking spine. The walking identity line
itself renders GREEN on the spine.

**Shell N10 — THE BUTTON.**
> Same walk, [PRODUCT (size)] raised to the lens, held. Delivery: full stop. He says:
> "And that's exactly what [PRODUCT] delivers." (tail 0.6s)

**VERDICT: GREEN** — a hold at lens distance with the real product photo attached; keep
the packet at arm's length — pushing it into macro range trades the label into the
fine-print ban. This is the ONLY legal long hold in the format (1.88s + the 0.6s tail).

## How Ad N banks into 4–9s generations (corrected under the CONSERVATION RULE)

The original packing put N04+N05 in one gen (a walk beat + 3 macros = 4 sub-shots) — over
the 2–3 sub-shot ceiling. The conservation rule: a setup displaced by the ceiling (or by a
RED verdict) is recovered as an extra generation or a still flash, NEVER silently deleted.
The fill must land the winner's measured 25-shot density from ~6–8 gens + the still suite.

### The generations (7)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 hook+break | N01+N02 (hook merges forward) | 2: cameo question → packet-gone break | 22 | ~3.9s | 5s (hook action buffer) |
| G2 the rant | N03 | 1 continuous take (take-jumps manufactured edit-side) | 32 | ~6.4s | 7s |

## How the edit reaches 25 shots (edit-grammar hooks)

- **A generation is raw footage, never a timeline shot.** The spine gen(s) slice into
  multiple NON-ADJACENT timeline segments across the 5 spine returns — different
  sub-ranges, no frame reuse — interleaved with the inserts: spine→insert→spine→insert.
- **Insert suites assemble as punch-in overlays / still flashes** over the continuous VO
  at word onsets; the host clip's audio never stops, overlay slices are always mute. The
  word-action locks are EDIT-side guarantees on the whisper timeline, never render
  promises.
- **Any stretch running >4s without a cut takes a flash or a crop punch-in** — a ~1s still
  flash is too brief to expose stillness.
- **The stitcher QC reads this bank's rhythm card:** median ≤1.5s, no shot >4s except the
  button close, ~0.7 cuts/sec, acceleration at the turn.
- **The second-winner slot (first-person confession) STAYS OPEN** — its rhythm card and
  shells are added when the member banks that winner.
- Mechanics — the two rails, the shot-level EDL, overlay assembly, sub-shot
  reconciliation, the cut-rhythm QC — live in `render-laws.md`; this bank supplies the
  targets, not the machinery.

## Per-format intake list (DIRECT-TO-CAMERA NARRATIVE)

Collected at intake, enforced before Gate 2. No photo → that beat becomes a still-insert
from what exists, or is cut. **Never invented.**

1. **Face + body refs.** ONE body reference per distinct outfit shown — the proof-suite
   eating beat in a second outfit needs its own ref or it collapses to the first outfit
   (lighting/framing variation instead).
2. **Product photo + every shown variant/flavor's own photo.** The packet color run needs
   one real photo per color — no shared ref, no recolors.
3. **Every printed face of the packaging + the nutrition-label photo.** Label beats are
   still-inserts from real photos ONLY (fine-print macro ban).
4. **Product dimensions** (stated inline in every prompt that shows the product).
5. **Voice clip ≤15s** (per-gen unique cuts per `voice-and-parallel.md`).
6. **Sanctioned claims** + the true story behind the confession/identity close.
7. **The real offer, if any** (otherwise the close stays identity + button and the offer
   rides the ad copy).

## Cross-source laws for the DIRECT-TO-CAMERA NARRATIVE format (what a fill must keep)

1. **Problem before product.** The product may CAMEO in the hook (2s, no pitch, no name
   unless the hook line needs it) but is absent through the whole problem section and
   never pitched before the turn.
2. **The turn lands between 25% and 35%** of the runtime, in one short sentence — and cut
   density DOUBLES there (the rhythm card's acceleration law).
3. **Motion carries the talking.** The spine is a walk (or an equivalently moving frame);
   static couch delivery is a different format. Background change per beat = the scene
   ladder for free.
4. **Every claim gets a simultaneous proof insert** — sensory→macro, mechanism→real-label
   still, lifestyle→action b-roll, range→the stack still. The VO never claims what the
   picture isn't showing; fidelity-critical inserts come from real photos.
5. **At least two word-action locks**, placed edit-side at word onsets and checkable
   against the whisper timeline.
6. **Category attacks stay generic** (air quotes, "most X"), never a named competitor;
   label facts are the member's sanctioned claims from real packaging photos.
7. **The close is identity + button** (or the member's real offer if they have one) — the
   ad ends on the walk with the product at the lens, ~0.6s tail.
8. **Pace ~4.0 wps** (measured), 30–40s, ~6–8 generations + the still-insert suite, cut to
   the rhythm card's targets. The batch mode applies: 4 unique hooks + the insert ladder
   over this shared body, per this skill's `variant-fanout.md`.
9. **Second-person editorial is the banked voice; first-person confession is the open
   slot** — when the member banks a confession-story winner, it completes this file.
10. **Ships clean.** The source's captions and callout stickers are editor-side; ours
    never render text — and because the source's sticker pops did rhythmic work, our edit
    needs every real cut more than the source did.
